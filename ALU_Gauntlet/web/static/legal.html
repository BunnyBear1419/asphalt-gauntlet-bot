01 Terms of Service

Use the RSL website, player systems, competition tools, and community features responsibly and according to rules published by RSL staff.

Accounts & access

RSL may use Discord authentication to identify an account. Users are responsible for maintaining control of their Discord account and activity performed through their authenticated account.

Acceptable use

  • Do not intentionally abuse, disrupt, or bypass website or competition systems.
  • Do not impersonate another player, staff member, club, or organization.
  • Do not submit knowingly false competition records or evidence.
  • Do not distribute malicious, unlawful, or abusive material.
This Legal Center is a policy overview and is not a substitute for advice from a qualified attorney.

02 Privacy

RSL may process information needed to provide authenticated website and community features. Depending on the feature, this can include Discord account identifiers, display information, player profiles, club membership, competition records, preferences, and technical session information.

How information is used

  • Authenticate users and provide account features.
  • Operate Gauntlet and tournament systems.
  • Maintain player, club, match, and ranking records.
  • Save website preferences such as language settings.
  • Protect the service and investigate abuse or administrative issues.

Google Analytics

RSL uses Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use the website, measure traffic and improve site content and functionality. Google Analytics may use cookies or similar identifiers and receives measurement information from pages that use the Google tag. RSL does not intentionally send personally identifiable information to Google Analytics.

Google provides information about how it processes data from sites that use its services and provides controls for users who want to limit Analytics measurement.

Data questions

For questions about information associated with an RSL account, contact RSL administrators through the official support process. Legally required requests will be handled according to applicable law.

03 Cookies & Storage

The website can use browser cookies and local browser storage for authentication, language preferences, and functionality needed to operate consistently.

Language

The language selector may save your selected language so it can be restored on later visits.

Authentication

Login/session information may be stored in browser cookies so authenticated pages can recognize your session.

Analytics

Google Analytics 4 may use first-party analytics cookies or similar identifiers to measure visits and interactions. These are used for site measurement and improvement, not for RSL account authentication.

Cookie controls

Users can manage cookies through their browser settings, including blocking or deleting cookies. Browser controls vary by browser and device. RSL may also provide an on-site cookie preference control where required or supported; changing cookie settings can affect features that depend on cookies, including authenticated sessions and saved preferences.

Cookie and privacy requirements can vary by location. Where applicable law requires consent or another user choice before analytics storage or measurement, RSL should provide the required control before enabling those technologies.

04 Community & Conduct

RSL is intended to provide an organized competitive racing community. Members are expected to communicate respectfully and follow RSL staff and Discord community rules.

  • No harassment, threats, targeted abuse, or impersonation.
  • No deliberate cheating, exploitation, or manipulation of competition systems.
  • No fraudulent match results, fabricated evidence, or ranking manipulation.
  • Staff may review reports and apply community or competition actions under published rules.

05 Competition Rules

Gauntlet and tournament participation is governed by RSL competition rules. Registration, eligibility, match reporting, evidence, deadlines, disputes, and administrative corrections may vary by event or season.

Records & results

Competition records may be stored as part of player career and ranking history. Administrative corrections may be made when a result is invalid, duplicated, or incorrectly recorded.

Disputes

Players should use the designated RSL staff/support process for match disputes rather than attempting unauthorized changes to website or database records.

07A Security

RSL uses authentication, access controls, and server-side protections intended to protect the website and its stored application data. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

If you discover a security issue, do not attempt to access another user’s information. Report the issue privately to RSL staff through the official support process.

07B Privacy Choices

Users can review the language and account preferences available through the website. Requests concerning personal information should be directed to RSL administrators through the official support process and will be handled according to applicable law.

08 Website Accessibility

RSL aims to keep its website usable across desktop and mobile devices and to provide clear navigation, readable content, keyboard-friendly controls, and meaningful labels where practical.

If you encounter an accessibility problem, report the page and describe what prevented you from using it so the RSL team can investigate.
