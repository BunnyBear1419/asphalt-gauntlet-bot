06 Gauntlet & Tournament Rules

RSL competition rules are community rules for organized Asphalt Legends Unite racing. They are separate from Gameloft's official game terms and any rules published for an individual official esports event. Asphalt Legends Unite supports cross-platform competition and private lobbies, including configurable race settings and spectators, which makes it suitable for organized community events.

Gauntlet Rules

  • Players must register before participating in an RSL Gauntlet season or match.
  • Use the registered player/account identity for all official matches.
  • Match results and required evidence must be submitted accurately and within the event deadline.
  • Players must follow the published car, class, track, mode, and other restrictions for the specific Gauntlet season.
  • No cheating, exploits, unauthorized software, fabricated results, or deliberate manipulation of rankings.
  • Players should complete matches respectfully and resolve disputes through RSL staff rather than altering records themselves.

Tournament Rules

  • Players and teams must meet the tournament's published eligibility and registration requirements.
  • Bracket, match, team-size, reporting, and deadline rules are determined by each tournament configuration.
  • Players must use the approved race settings and submit accurate results/evidence.
  • Intentional cheating or match manipulation is prohibited.
  • Harassment, hate speech, threats, doxxing, and disruptive conduct are not permitted. These principles are also reflected in official Asphalt esports competition rules.
  • RSL staff may review disputes, correct invalid or duplicated records, and apply competition actions according to the published event rules.
RSL rules govern RSL community events. Official Gameloft/Asphalt esports events can have their own rules, eligibility requirements, penalties, and codes of conduct.

07 What the Website & Discord Bot Do

Racing Syndicate League is designed as a connected web and Discord competition platform for the Asphalt Legends Unite community. The website acts as the visual control center, while Discord provides the community and operational side of the league.

Website capabilities

  • Player profiles, driver information, garage/career views and account-linked settings.
  • Gauntlet registration, defense, challenges & matches, leaderboards and references.
  • Tournament registration, brackets & matches, results/rankings and club tournaments.
  • Club management and club competition features.
  • Calendar views for league and tournament events.
  • Search, Help Center, Legal Center, account settings and language preferences.
  • Staff administration for server setup, competition management, records, and league configuration.

Discord bot capabilities

  • Provides the Discord-side interface for RSL community operations.
  • Supports dashboard and staff workflows while keeping the visible command surface focused.
  • Handles competition-related registration, match/result workflows, announcements, logging, and administrative processes as configured by staff.
  • Connects Discord identity with website account/profile functionality where authentication is enabled.
  • Supports server setup and staff-controlled configuration for channels, roles, competition systems, and notifications.
  • Works with the league database so competition records and player/club information can be used across the connected web and Discord experience.
The website and bot are RSL community tools. They do not modify Asphalt Legends Unite itself or provide access to Gameloft's internal game systems.
