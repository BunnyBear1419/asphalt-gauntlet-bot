Racing Syndicate League

Race Fair. Compete Clean. Respect the Community.

This is the central rulebook for RSL community competition. Event-specific rules can add requirements, deadlines, race settings, eligibility rules, or penalties for a particular season or tournament.

01 Community Conduct

  • Treat other drivers, staff, clubs, and community members respectfully.
  • No harassment, threats, targeted abuse, hate speech, doxxing, or impersonation.
  • Do not intentionally disrupt RSL services, competition channels, or administrative processes.
  • Follow reasonable staff directions during an active competition or review.

02 Gauntlet Rules

  • Register for the applicable Gauntlet season before participating in official Gauntlet competition.
  • Use the registered player identity for official matches.
  • Follow the season's published division, car, class, track, mode, race-count, and other restrictions.
  • Report match results accurately through the approved process.
  • Do not manipulate matchmaking, tickets, refreshes, points, ELO, records, or settlement behavior.
  • Abandoned, quit, duplicate, or replayed matches are subject to the settlement and recovery rules.
  • Free and paid ticket availability follows the current daily economy; unused tickets do not carry over.

03 Tournament Rules

  • Meet the tournament's published eligibility and registration requirements.
  • Follow the configured bracket, player/team format, race settings, deadlines, and result-reporting process.
  • Only approved tournament results are used for tournament records and rankings.
  • Do not intentionally manipulate brackets, results, rankings, or match verification.
  • Tournament-specific rules may establish additional penalties, evidence requirements, or deadlines.

04 Matches & Results

  • Report the actual result of the race or match.
  • Submit required evidence when the event or staff process requires it.
  • Do not submit duplicate results or replay a settled match as a new competitive result.
  • If a match is interrupted, abandoned, or disputed, use the published recovery/support process rather than creating a second record.
  • Administrative corrections may be made when records are invalid, duplicated, or incorrectly recorded.

05 Fair Play & Exploits

  • No cheating, unauthorized software, fabricated evidence, deliberate ranking manipulation, or exploitation of known system defects.
  • Do not bypass ticket, refresh, registration, settlement, authentication, or access controls.
  • Do not use another player's account or impersonate another driver.
  • If you discover a serious exploit, stop using it competitively and report it privately to staff.

06 Tournament Media

  • Only submit media you have the right to share.
  • Follow tournament-specific media requirements and submission channels.
  • Player-submitted screenshots/videos may require staff approval before publication.
  • Do not submit illegal, abusive, private, or unrelated content.

07 Disputes & Staff Decisions

Use the official RSL Discord ticket channel for match, ranking, account, conduct, technical, or other disputes and support requests. Provide the relevant season/tournament, match information, and evidence when available.

Staff may investigate reports, review evidence, correct invalid records, restore a recoverable match state, or apply competition/community actions under the applicable rules.

08 Rule Changes & Event-Specific Rules

RSL may update community and competition rules as the platform evolves. Rules published for a specific season or tournament may add requirements that apply only to that event.

These are RSL community rules. They do not replace Gameloft/Asphalt terms, official game rules, or rules for official esports competitions.
