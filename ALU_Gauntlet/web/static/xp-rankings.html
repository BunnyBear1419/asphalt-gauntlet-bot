XP & Rankings

Advanced community progression is now part of RSL. Message, voice and reaction activity can contribute to XP, with configurable curves, boosters, weekly and monthly progression, and detailed rankings.

Everything is free

XP Progression

Linear, exponential and flat curves are supported. XP boosters can be configured by role or channel, with cooldowns and activity protections.

Rankings

All-time, weekly and monthly XP rankings are available alongside voice activity, reaction activity and RSL Activity.

RSL Fairness

XP is community progression only. It does not change Gauntlet matchmaking, division placement, race scoring or tournament competitive outcomes.

Leaderboard API

The live leaderboard is available to the signed-in RSL website and supports all-time, weekly and monthly views.
